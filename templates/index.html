What this version can establish

Aegis detects patterns in recorded honeypot activity. It does not establish production compromise, malware execution, or C2. The synthetic evaluation checks pipeline behavior; it does not measure real-world accuracy.

Reports are generated from rules by default. When a local model is configured, its draft is displayed separately for analyst review.
